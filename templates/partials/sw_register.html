{# Registers the service worker. Put this at the END of on every page. Registration is deliberately late and deliberately silent. It is not required for the page to work, so it must never be able to break one: a browser without service worker support, a page served over plain HTTP, or a registration that simply fails all end up doing nothing at all rather than throwing. NOTE ON http:// — service workers only run on https or on localhost. On a LAN address over plain http the registration is skipped by the browser and offline support silently does not exist. That is not a bug in this file; it is why the roadmap puts a real domain with TLS at trigger T1. #}
