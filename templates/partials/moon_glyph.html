{#- The OVERNIGHT glyph. One file, same reasoning as the two arrows: a second layover marker drawn somewhere else would drift, which is how the aeroplane became three silhouettes before 1.7.0. A crescent rather than a bed or a moon-and-stars, because it has to read at 13px in --muted against --rest. Anything with interior detail turns into a smudge at that size. No width/height attributes — sized by CSS (.overnight-icon svg), so the same file can serve a larger layover marker if the calendar ever grows one. Stroke is lighter than the arrows': those are painted on a saturated disc, this one sits on a quiet band and must not shout. -#}
