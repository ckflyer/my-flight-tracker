{#- The SHARE glyph. One file, same reasoning as the arrows and the moon: a second share icon drawn somewhere else would drift. The iOS-style tray-and-arrow rather than the three-connected-dots Android mark, because the button's main job on a phone is opening the OS share sheet and the tray reads as that on both platforms. No width/height attributes — sized by CSS, so one file serves any button that needs it. -#}
