Getting your OpenSky credentials

  1. Go to opensky-network.org and create a free account (or log in if you already have one).
  2. Once logged in, open your Account page.
  3. Find the API Client section and generate a new client — this gives you a Client ID and a Client Secret.
  4. Copy both values.
  5. Paste them into this app's Settings page, in the "OpenSky credentials" section, and save.

This is what powers live ADS-B tracking (position, altitude, speed) for your current flight. Without it, the app still works for your schedule and tracking links — you just won't see the live map until credentials are added.
